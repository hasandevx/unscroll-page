Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Unscroll is designed as a privacy-first, serverless application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), location data, app usage habits, or contact information.

2. Local Storage & Encryption

All focus metrics, session logs, and blocked app preferences are stored exclusively on your Android device in an encrypted SQLCipher database and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. No external servers have access to this key or data.

3. Accessibility & Usage Stats Permissions

Unscroll requires Android Accessibility and Usage Access permissions solely to detect short-form video feeds (such as Instagram Reels or TikTok) and display the friction intervention screen. This data is processed locally on-device and is never logged or transmitted anywhere.

4. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable backup, data is stored in your personal Google Drive account's isolated AppData folder. We do not have access to your Google Drive or any files within it.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Telemetry

We use Aptabase for anonymous, aggregate crash diagnostics. Aptabase is an open-source, privacy-first service that does not track IP addresses, device identifiers, or user identities.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at privacy@unscroll.app.
