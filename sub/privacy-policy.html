Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Unscroll mobile application for Android devices, operated by Sajjad Movahhedi ("Service Provider"). Unscroll is built with a zero-compromise, privacy-first, serverless architecture.

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Unscroll does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), device IP addresses, location data, contact lists, browsing history, or app usage habits to central servers. Your app usage stats and focus session logs remain 100% on your local Android device.

2. Local Storage & Database Encryption

All focus session metrics, blocked app settings, and streak data are stored exclusively on your Android device inside an encrypted SQLCipher database and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. No external servers or third parties hold access keys to your local data.

3. Android Accessibility & Usage Access Permissions

Unscroll utilizes Android's native Accessibility Service and Usage Access permissions solely to detect when short-form video feeds (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or TikTok) are opened on your phone, allowing the app to launch a calm mindfulness breath intervention screen. This detection is performed entirely on-device in real-time and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.

4. Optional Google Drive AppData Sync

If you explicitly enable backup functionality, your focus data is encrypted and synced exclusively to your personal Google Drive account's private AppData folder. The Service Provider has zero access to your Google account, Google Drive, or any synced backup files.

5. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

To support app functionality, crash reporting, and in-app subscriptions, Unscroll integrates with privacy-focused third-party SDKs. You can view their individual privacy practices below:

  • Aptabase — Privacy-first, open-source telemetry and anonymous crash diagnostics. Aptabase does NOT collect IP addresses, advertising IDs, or personal data.
  • RevenueCat — Secure in-app purchase and subscription validation via Google Play Billing.

6. All-Ages & Family Friendly Compliance

Unscroll is designed for users of all ages, including students, children, and adults seeking digital wellness and focus tools. Because Unscroll collects zero personal data, IP addresses, or user profiles, users of all ages can use the application safely without privacy risks. If a parent or guardian has any questions, they may reach out at hasan.movahhedi.developer@gmail.com.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Because your data is stored 100% locally on your phone, uninstalling the Unscroll application immediately erases all local data and database files from your device. If you enabled Google Drive backup, you can delete your backup folder directly inside your Google Drive settings at any time.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Service Provider via email at hasan.movahhedi.developer@gmail.com.
