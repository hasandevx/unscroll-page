Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Unscroll mobile application for Android devices, operated by Sajjad Movahhedi ("Service Provider"). Unscroll is built with a zero-compromise, 100% privacy-first, serverless architecture.


Zero-Server Architecture & Data Collection

Unscroll does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), device IP addresses, location data, contact lists, browsing history, or app usage habits to central servers. All app usage stats, blocked app preferences, and focus session logs remain 100% on your local Android device.


Local Database & SQLCipher Encryption

All focus session metrics, intervention counters, and streak data are stored exclusively on your device inside an encrypted SQLCipher database and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. No external servers hold decryption keys to your local data.


Android Accessibility & Usage Permissions

Unscroll utilizes Android's native Accessibility Service and Usage Access permissions solely to detect when short-form video feeds (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or TikTok) are opened, allowing the app to launch a calm mindfulness breath intervention. This detection is executed entirely on-device in real-time and is never logged, saved, or transmitted anywhere.


Optional Google Drive AppData Sync

If you explicitly enable backup functionality, your focus metrics are encrypted and synced exclusively to your personal Google Drive account's isolated AppData folder. The Service Provider has zero access to your Google account, Google Drive, or any backup files.


Third Party SDKs & Telemetry

Unscroll utilizes privacy-focused third-party SDKs for anonymous crash diagnostics and subscription processing. Below are their respective privacy policy links:

  • Aptabase — Privacy-first, open-source telemetry and anonymous crash diagnostics. Aptabase does NOT track IP addresses, advertising IDs, or personal data.
  • RevenueCat — Secure in-app purchase and subscription validation via Google Play Billing.

Your Rights & Data Control

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data held by the Service Provider by contacting hasan.movahhedi.developer@gmail.com.


Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to request deletion of personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact hasan.movahhedi.developer@gmail.com.


International Data Transfers & GDPR

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the Service Provider and its third-party service providers apply appropriate mechanisms under GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission and adequacy decisions.


Data Retention & Deletion

Because Unscroll operates serverlessly on your phone, uninstalling the Application immediately erases all local database files from your device. If you enabled Google Drive backup, you can delete your backup folder directly within your Google Drive settings at any time.


Children & All-Ages Privacy

Unscroll is designed for users of all ages, including children, students, and adults seeking digital wellness tools. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect, solicit, or harvest personal data from children or users of any age. If a parent or guardian has questions, please contact hasan.movahhedi.developer@gmail.com.


Security Safeguards

The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect confidentiality. All local data is protected by Android system sandbox isolation and SQLCipher encryption.

If a data breach affects any personal data processed by our third-party infrastructure, the Service Provider will notify users in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
